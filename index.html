04 / METHOD

What the score means

The score is editorial, reproducible, and explicit about uncertainty. It is not an objective measure of taste.

30

Design fit

How directly the skill supports poster, brand, editorial, advertising, or adjacent visual-design work.

25

Codex fit

Native tool usage, valid SKILL.md structure, install clarity, and Desktop compatibility.

20

Public feedback

Independent reviews, community cases, registry installs, and scoped adoption signals.

15

Maintenance

Recency of repository activity. Archived or stale projects decay gradually rather than disappearing.

10

Docs & examples

Installation steps, prompt recipes, real output examples, constraints, and quality gates.

IMPORTANT CAVEAT

Stars and forks are adoption proxies, not satisfaction scores. Host repositories containing many skills receive a scope discount so their total popularity cannot overwhelm a focused standalone skill.
